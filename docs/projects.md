## Research

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-graph-outline:{ .lg .middle } **MultiAffinity**

    ---

    <span class="card-meta">MSc thesis, 2022 · Python, R, Docker, Conda, MkDocs</span>

    A command line tool for studying how gene dysregulation propagates across a
    multilayer network, built to surface the key genes in a disease of interest.
    Applied to hepatoblastoma, an uncommon liver cancer usually diagnosed under
    age five: a meta-analysis of five public RNA-Seq studies (181 samples, 101
    patients) found 199 up- and 124 down-regulated genes, and a network spanning
    metabolic reactions, liver protein-protein interactions and pathways
    surfaced four candidates (`LRRC52`, `DLX6`, `DLX5`, `MSX2`) linked to the
    β-catenin pathway.

    Packaged with Docker and Conda, documented with MkDocs, released under
    AGPL-3.0, and deployed as a code-free tool to the Virtual Research
    Environment of the EU H2020 project iPC (ref. 826121).

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source on GitHub](https://github.com/marbatlle/multiAffinity)<br>
    [:octicons-link-external-16: Tool documentation](https://marbatlle.github.io/multiAffinity/)<br>
    [:material-file-pdf-box: Read the thesis (PDF)](files/msc-thesis.pdf)

-   :material-dna:{ .lg .middle } **Design and optimization of primers for gene transcript and methylation analysis on bovine embryos**

    ---

    <span class="card-meta">BSc thesis, 2018 · RT-qPCR, bisulfite sequencing</span>

    Carried out within a Ghent University project on sperm stressors and their
    effect on sperm methylation and subsequent embryo development in cattle. I
    designed, tested and optimized primers for embryo transcript expression
    analysis and for bisulfite-treated embryos, validating specificity and
    efficiency via RT-qPCR, melt curve analysis, agarose gel electrophoresis and
    Sanger sequencing.

    [:octicons-link-external-16: Read the thesis](http://hdl.handle.net/10256/16406)
