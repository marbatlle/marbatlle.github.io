# Projects

## Research

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-graph-outline:{ .lg .middle } **Multilayer networks for the molecular characterization of hepatoblastoma**

    ---

    MSc thesis, 2022. Hepatoblastoma is an uncommon liver cancer usually
    diagnosed under age five, with a largely unknown origin. This work
    characterized the landscape of high-throughput transcriptomics data in the
    disease through a meta-analysis of five publicly available RNA-Seq studies
    (181 samples, 101 patients), identifying 199 up- and 124 down-regulated
    genes. A multilayer network spanning metabolic reactions, liver
    protein-protein interactions and pathways was built to find the genes
    driving the spread of dysregulation, surfacing four candidates (`LRRC52`,
    `DLX6`, `DLX5`, `MSX2`) linked to the β-catenin pathway. The workflow was
    deployed as a code-free tool to the Virtual Research Environment of the EU
    H2020 project iPC (ref. 826121).

    [:material-file-pdf-box: Read the thesis (PDF)](files/msc-thesis.pdf)

-   :material-dna:{ .lg .middle } **Design and optimization of primers for gene transcript and methylation analysis on bovine embryos**

    ---

    BSc thesis, 2018. Carried out as part of an ongoing Ghent University project
    on sperm stressors and their effect on sperm methylation and subsequent
    embryo development in cattle. Designed, tested and optimized primers for
    embryo transcript expression analysis and for bisulfite-treated embryos,
    validating specificity and efficiency via RT-qPCR, melt curve analysis,
    agarose gel electrophoresis and Sanger sequencing.

    [:octicons-link-external-24: Read the thesis](http://hdl.handle.net/10256/16406)
